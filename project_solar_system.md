---

### Implementation Status: Completed 🎉
The interactive web application has been built and verified in [`index.html`](file:///Users/subratapaul/Documents/All_gits/ToruLearn/index.html).
- **Solar System Orrery**: 8 major planets + Pluto + asteroid belt with Keplerian motion and planet inspector.
- **How Day & Night Happen**: 24h rotating Earth on 23.5° axis, twilight terminator line, city night lights, and live ground observer sky preview (New York, London, Tokyo, Sydney, Cairo, North Pole).
- **How Seasons Happen**: Earth's fixed axial tilt pointing toward Polaris, direct vs angled sunlight, milestone solstices/equinoxes, and tilt experimentation slider (0° to 45°).
- **How Moon Phases Happen**: Synchronized dual view (top-down space view where 50% is always lit vs view from Earth sky), 29.5-day synodic scrubber, and tidal locking explanation.
- **How Eclipses Happen**: Solar and Lunar eclipse demonstrator with umbra/penumbra shadow cones, 5.1° orbital tilt toggle, and real-time totality telescope (solar corona, diamond ring, blood moon Rayleigh scattering).
- **Cosmic Master Quiz**: 10 astrophysics questions with explanations and ranking badges.
- **Cosmic Audio**: Procedural Web Audio API soundscape and interactive effects.
